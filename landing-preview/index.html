Philosophy

Debt is rarely as large as the silence around it. We believe people deserve to look at their numbers directly — without shame, without sales pitches, without software that wants something from them.

— A note from the founder

Three steps.
One quiet afternoon.

Most people avoid their debt because seeing it feels worse than not knowing. We designed each step to feel like the opposite: deliberate, calm, and entirely under your control.

i. Upload

Bring your credit report

Download your free report from annualcreditreport.com — the only federally authorized source. Upload the PDF. We never ask for your social security number or credit card. There is nothing to buy.

ii. Organize

Watch it become legible

Every account, every collection, every charge-off — extracted, sorted, and traced from the original creditor to whoever holds the debt today. You see the full chain, not the fragments.

iii. Decide

Choose your path forward

Compare payoff strategies side by side. See your debt-free date. Understand what each minimum payment is actually doing. No advisor, no upsell — just the math, presented honestly.

Our promise

What we will never do.

i.

Sell your data

Your credit information is not for sale, not for analysis, and not for partner integrations. It is yours alone.

ii.

Refer you to a debt-relief company

No affiliates. No commissions. No recommended partners. We make money in exactly one way, and it has nothing to do with your debt.

iii.

Pretend to be your friend

No motivational notifications. No streaks. No gamification. This is a tool, not a relationship.

iv.

Hold your data hostage

Export everything as a PDF or spreadsheet at any time. Delete your account with one click and we mean it — gone, permanently, within twenty-four hours.

Begin where you actually are.

Not where the credit card companies want you to be. Not where the apps with bright colors say you should be. Where you are.
